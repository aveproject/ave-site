Crosswalks

How other scanners' detection categories and the frameworks security teams already use map to AVE behavioral classification ids, so findings from different tools become comparable through a shared vocabulary.

Each crosswalk maps another tool's or framework's categories onto AVE records, or AVE's onto theirs. Open one to see its mappings, the notes behind them and the gaps on either side, or search every mapping on the page at once. A link to crosswalks.html#xw-<file name> opens that crosswalk.

Maintain a scanner? Map your rules to AVE ids, or open an issue and the maintainer will help build the crosswalk.

AVE is an open standard. The schema and records are published under Apache 2.0. AVE is moving toward neutral governance under an independent standards body, meant to outlive any single implementation, including Bawbel's.

Maps to OWASP MCP Top 10 · OWASP Agentic AI Top 10 · MITRE ATLAS · OWASP AIVSS v0.8. The open record set can be consumed by any tool.

Citing AVE. Reference a record by its id, which is permanent and never reused: AVE-YYYY-NNNNN: Title. Agentic Vulnerability Enumeration (AVE). https://aveproject.org/registry.html#AVE-YYYY-NNNNN
